TrueLevel

A spirit level for Android

  • Reads like a real level. A horizontal vial, a vertical vial and a bull's eye, in the same place in every pose, with lines that mark ±0.05°.
  • Calibrates properly. Guided reversal calibration corrects each side of the phone, camera bump and case included, on a surface that doesn't need to be level.
  • Built for the job. Five units, four vial ranges, hold, lock and zero, a 45° screen, and haptic and audio feedback.
  • Keeps a record. Save readings with notes and export them as a CSV file. No account needed.
