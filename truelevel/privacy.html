TrueLevel Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the TrueLevel Android app (package com.varun.truelevel) handles your information. TrueLevel is a spirit level published on Google Play by TrueLevel ("we", "us").

In short
  • TrueLevel has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. We do not receive your measurements.
  • Your readings, notes, settings and calibration are stored on your phone.
  • The free version shows adverts through Google AdMob. Google's advertising software collects device and usage data, including your advertising ID, as described in section 3.
  • Premium is optional, is paid through Google Play, and removes adverts. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your information.

1. What is stored on your phone

TrueLevel keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • Measurement history: the date and time, the angles, the phone's orientation, any note you type, and whether the reading was saved automatically. The log keeps your latest 500 readings.
  • Settings: your unit, vial range, damping, theme and feedback choices.
  • Calibration: the correction values measured for your phone's sensors and sides.
  • Premium status: your plan, and the serial numbers of any redeem codes used, so Premium works offline.

The app reads your phone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) to measure angles. Those readings are processed on your phone. We do not receive any of this data, and the app does not pass your measurements or notes to the advertising software.

2. Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include your measurement history, settings and Premium status in your device backup with Google, and in a transfer to a new phone. This is a feature of Android that you control, and we cannot access those backups. Calibration is excluded from both, because it describes one physical phone.

3. Adverts

The free version shows adverts provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google, not us, collects and processes this data. According to Google's published disclosure, the SDK may collect and share:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with adverts and the app, such as launches, taps and views;
  • diagnostic information, such as crashes and performance;
  • device and account identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and it is encrypted in transit. Adverts may be personalised unless you opt out or the law in your region limits this. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Privacy › Ads, and manage personalised adverts at My Ad Center. Premium removes adverts.

4. Premium purchases

Premium is optional and is sold through Google Play, under Google Payments' privacy notice. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

  • To know whether you have Premium, the app asks Google Play on your phone which purchases your account holds.
  • Google shares order details with us in the Google Play Console, such as the order number, date, product, price and country, so we can handle refunds and support requests.
  • Redeem codes are checked on your phone. Nothing about a code is sent to us.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play subscriptions.

5. Exporting and sharing

You can export your history as a CSV file through Android's share menu, or copy it to the clipboard. It goes only where you send it, and the receiving app's privacy policy then applies.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and we hold no copy of your measurements. In the app you can delete single readings or clear the whole history. Clearing TrueLevel's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything on your phone. Android backups are managed in your Google account. Data collected by Google for adverts is handled under Google's policies and controls linked in section 3.

8. Google Play

If you installed TrueLevel from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and, if you have agreed in your device settings, crash and performance reports, under Google's Privacy Policy. We may see these as aggregated statistics in the Google Play Console.

9. Security

Data on your phone stays inside Android's app sandbox and is protected by your phone's own encryption and screen lock. Traffic to Google's advertising services is encrypted in transit.

10. Children

TrueLevel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU or UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of personalised adverts. Everything TrueLevel stores is visible and deletable in the app as described above, and Google's controls are linked in section 3. For any request or complaint, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how TrueLevel handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
