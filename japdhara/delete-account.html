Delete your JapDhara account

JapDhara: Naam Jap Counter, by JapDhara

You only have a JapDhara account if you signed in with Google under More › Sync. If you never signed in, there is no account and nothing of yours is stored by us: all your data is on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open JapDhara and go to More › Sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to choose your Google account again.

Your synced data and your account are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. Send an email to varunbansal1409@gmail.com with the subject Delete my JapDhara account.
  2. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, or include that account's email address, so we can find the right account.

We delete the account and all of its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: user ID, email address, name and profile picture link.
  • Everything synced to it: jap sittings, mantras (including those you wrote), sankalpa vows and their notes, and deletion markers.

Nothing is kept afterwards, and the deletion cannot be undone. Your settings, pictures, recordings and voice templates were never uploaded, so there is nothing of them to delete on our side.

What stays on your phone

Deleting the account does not touch the data on your phone, so you can keep practising offline. To remove that too, use Backup › Clear jap history and Delete all recordings in the app, clear JapDhara's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app. Any backup files you exported yourself are yours to delete.

Delete some data but keep the account

Delete individual sittings or mantras in the app, then tap Sync now: the deletion is carried to your other devices. To have specific synced items removed from the cloud without deleting your account, email us as in option 2 and say which.
