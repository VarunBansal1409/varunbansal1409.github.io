Delete your JapDhara account

JapDhara: Naam Jap Counter, by JapDhara

You only have a JapDhara account if you signed in with Google under More › Sync. If you never signed in, there is no account and nothing of yours is stored by us: all your data is on your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open JapDhara and go to More › Sync.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm, then choose the same Google account again when asked.

Your synced data and your account are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. From the Gmail address of the Google account you signed in with, send an email to varunbansal1409@gmail.com with the subject Delete my JapDhara account.
  2. We send a confirmation request to that account's address. Reply to it from that address.

To protect you, we only act on a request confirmed from the account's own address. We then delete the account and all of its data within 30 days of your confirmation, and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account: user ID, email address, name and profile picture link.
  • Everything synced to it: jap sittings, mantras (including those you wrote), sankalpa vows and their notes, and deletion markers.

Nothing is kept on our side afterwards, and the deletion cannot be undone. Your settings, pictures, recordings and voice templates were never uploaded, so there is nothing of them to delete.

Using JapDhara on more than one device? Sign out on the others first (More › Sync › Sign out). A device that is still signed in could otherwise sync its copy back in the short time before it notices the account is gone. If that happens, email us as in option 2 and we will remove it.

Premium is not cancelled

A Premium subscription belongs to your Google Play account, so deleting your JapDhara account does not cancel it or refund it. To stop paying, cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

What stays on your phone

Deleting the account does not touch the data on your phone, so you can keep practising offline. To remove that too, use Backup › Clear jap history and Delete all recordings in the app, clear JapDhara's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app. Any backup files you exported yourself are yours to delete.

Delete some data but keep the account

Delete individual sittings or mantras in the app, then tap Sync now: the deletion is carried to your other devices. To have specific synced items removed from the cloud without deleting your account, email us as in option 2 and say which. The same confirmation applies.
