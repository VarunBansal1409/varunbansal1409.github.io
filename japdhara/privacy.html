JapDhara Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the JapDhara Android app (package com.japdhara.japdhara) handles your information. JapDhara is a naam jap and mantra counter published on Google Play by JapDhara ("we", "us").

In short
  • JapDhara works fully without an account. Your jap history, mantras, recordings and pictures are stored on your phone.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, your jap history, mantras and sankalpa vows are copied to our cloud database so they can sync between your devices. Only your account can read them.
  • Counting by voice listens on your phone. Your voice is never uploaded, and recordings you make never leave your phone unless you export a backup yourself.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time, from the app or by email.

1. Using JapDhara without an account

You can use every feature without signing in. Everything you create is kept in a private database inside the app's own storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read:

  • your jap sittings: the date and time, the mantra, the count, malas, the time spent and how you counted (tap, voice, handwriting and so on);
  • your mantras, including any you write yourself (name, text, meaning, deity) and which ones you starred;
  • your sankalpa vows (target, dates and any note you add), goals, streaks and achievements;
  • your settings, such as language, theme, reminders and app lock;
  • pictures and recordings you add to your mantras (see sections 3 and 4).

We do not receive any of this. It stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall JapDhara, which removes it all.

2. Optional Google sign-in and sync

Under More › Sync you can sign in with your Google account to keep your practice in step on several devices. Sync runs only when you sign in or tap Sync now. It uses two Google services that work for us as service providers: Firebase Authentication to sign you in and Cloud Firestore to store the synced copy.

What is stored in the cloud when you sign in

  • Account details: a user ID, and the email address, name and profile picture link that your Google account provides to Firebase Authentication. The app shows your email address on the Sync screen and uses the user ID to keep your data separate.
  • Jap sittings: date, time, mantra, count, malas, time spent and counting method.
  • Mantras: the mantra list with any mantras you wrote, their names, text and meaning, deity, colour, starred or archived state, and the file names of their picture and recording (not the files).
  • Sankalpa vows: target, dates, status and your note.
  • Deletion markers: a record of which items you deleted, so a deleted item is not copied back from another device.

Your settings, pictures, recordings and voice templates are not synced. Because your jap practice and the mantras you choose relate to your religious practice, please sync only if you are comfortable with that copy being kept in our cloud database.

The database's security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write your data. No other user can see it, and nothing is ever published. Google stores it on its servers, which may be outside your country, under the Firebase privacy and security terms.

Signing out keeps everything on your phone and leaves the cloud copy in place, so you can sign in again later. To remove the cloud copy, delete your account (section 9).

3. Microphone and your voice

JapDhara asks for the microphone only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • Counting by voice listens to your chanting and counts each repetition of the mantra. The sound is analysed on your phone by a speech model running inside the app, as you chant. It is not saved and is never sent anywhere.
  • Teaching a mantra to voice counting saves a small numeric pattern of how you chant that mantra, plus the text the speech model heard, so it can recognise the mantra next time. It is not a recording, it stays on your phone, it is not synced and it is not in backups. You can delete it from the mantra's voice settings.
  • Recording a mantra in your own voice saves an audio file in the app's storage on your phone, used to recite the mantra in Voice jap and Automatic jap. It is not synced.

4. Pictures and audio files you add

You can give a mantra a picture, taken with your camera app or chosen from your gallery, and an audio file chosen from your phone. JapDhara uses Android's own pickers, so it only sees the item you pick. A resized copy of the picture, or a copy of the audio, is saved in the app's storage on your phone. Neither is uploaded.

5. The speech model download

Counting by voice needs a speech model of about 70 MB that is not built into the app. When you tap Download, JapDhara fetches it once over an encrypted connection from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), where the open-source model is published, and checks it against a known fingerprint before using it. The request contains no information about you or your practice. Like any website, Hugging Face and its content delivery network receive your IP address and basic request details, under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Nothing is sent to Hugging Face after the download.

6. Backups and sharing

  • Backup file. Backup › Export creates a .zip file containing your settings, mantras, jap history, sankalpa vows, recordings and pictures. It is not encrypted. It goes only where you send it from Android's share menu, and once saved it is in your control. Import reads a backup file you choose.
  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include JapDhara's data, such as your history, mantras, settings and media, in your device backup with Google. This is a feature of Android that you control, and we cannot access it. The speech model is excluded.
  • Progress cards. When you share a progress card, the picture (your count, malas, streak and mantra name) goes to the app you choose. It never includes your name or email address.

When you send something to another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

7. Other features

  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not send push notifications.
  • App lock uses Android's fingerprint, face or screen lock. JapDhara only learns whether you unlocked it, never the biometric itself. While the lock is on, JapDhara blocks screenshots and hides the count on the home-screen widget.
  • Voice jap without a recording reads the mantra aloud with your phone's text-to-speech engine. If you have chosen an engine that uses online voices, that engine's provider handles the text under its own policy.

9. Deleting your data and your account

  • On your phone: delete individual sittings or mantras, use Backup › Clear jap history or Delete all recordings, clear JapDhara's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Your account and cloud data: in the app, open More › Sync and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your synced data and your sign-in account. Data on your phone is kept until you delete it there.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our account deletion page. We delete the account and all of its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

We keep cloud data only while your account exists. We keep no copies after deletion, except where the law requires otherwise.

10. What we do not do

JapDhara has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above, who process it only to run the features you use.

11. Google Play

If you installed JapDhara from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and, if you have agreed in your device settings, crash and performance reports, under Google's Privacy Policy. We may see these as anonymous, aggregated statistics in the Google Play Console.

12. Security

Everything sent between the app and Google or Hugging Face is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud data is protected by access rules tied to your account. Data on your phone stays inside Android's app sandbox and is protected by your phone's own encryption and screen lock.

13. Children

JapDhara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has signed in to sync, contact us and we will delete the account.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can see everything JapDhara holds in the app, export it with Backup, stop syncing by signing out, and delete your account as described above. For any request or complaint, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change how JapDhara handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
