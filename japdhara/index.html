JapDhara

जपधारा · Naam jap and mantra counter

  • Count the way you chant. Tap anywhere, swipe down bead by bead, press the volume keys, write the name with your finger, or let JapDhara count your chanting by voice.
  • 108 mantras, and your own. Devanagari, transliteration and meaning for each, plus mantras you add with your own picture and recorded voice.
  • See your sadhana grow. Daily goals, streaks, records, a practice calendar, achievements and sankalpa vows with the daily pace to complete them.
  • Private by design. No account needed and no ads. Voice counting runs on your phone. Sign in with Google only if you want your history synced between devices.
