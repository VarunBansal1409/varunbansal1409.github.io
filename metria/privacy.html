Metria privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026

This policy explains how the Metria Android app (package com.metria.measure) handles your information. Metria is a measuring app published on Google Play by Metria ("we", "us").

In short
  • Your measurements, photos and camera images stay on your phone. We run no servers and never receive them.
  • You don't need an account.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to serve them.
  • Premium is an optional subscription paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. What the app uses on your phone

  • Camera. To measure in augmented reality, to measure with a bank card in Precision mode, and to calibrate the camera. Images are processed on your phone in real time and are not sent to us or to advertisers. They are not saved, except a photo you choose to keep with a measurement.
  • Motion sensors. These power the Level. Readings are used live and are not stored, apart from the Level's calibration on your phone.
  • Screen size. The Ruler uses your display's reported pixel density to turn pixels into millimetres.

2. What is stored on your phone

  • Measurements you save: the numbers, their accuracy, the time, any name you type, and optional annotated photos.
  • Camera calibrations, the Ruler's scale check and the Level's calibration.
  • Your settings, such as units and appearance, and whether you have Premium.

All of this lives in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. We have no copy of it. If you have turned on Android's own backup, your phone may include your saved measurements in that backup, which Google manages under your account. Photos and calibrations are excluded from it.

3. When your measurements leave your phone

Only when you choose to send them. If you tap Export or Share, Android shows you the apps you can send a file to, and the file goes only to the app you pick. Copying a reading puts it on your clipboard.

4. Advertising

The free version of Metria shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app collects the following automatically and sends it to Google:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • your device's Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • how you interact with the app and with ads;
  • diagnostic information about the app and the ads.

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice before ads load, and Metria's Settings let you change it. On any phone you can limit personalised ads or delete your Advertising ID in Android's settings, under Privacy, then Ads. The exact wording varies by phone.

Premium subscribers don't see ads.

5. Premium purchases

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription, sold and paid for entirely through Google Play, under Google Payments' privacy notice. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

  • To know whether you have Premium, the app asks Google Play, on your phone, which purchases your Google Play account holds. The answer is kept on your phone. The app sends nothing about your purchase to us.
  • Google shares order details with us in the Google Play Console, as it does with every developer, such as the order number, date, product, price and country, so we can handle refunds and support requests.
  • Premium belongs to your Google Play account. Uninstalling Metria does not cancel it. You manage or cancel it in Google Play.

6. Google Play Services for AR

The AR features run on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), a separate app that Google installs and updates. It is provided by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy. Metria does not receive or control any data it handles.

7. Google Play

If you installed Metria from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and crashes under its own policy, and shows us aggregated statistics in the Google Play Console.

8. Permissions

  • Camera. Asked for only when you open a screen that needs it.
  • Internet and network state. Used for ads and purchases, not for your measurements.
  • Advertising ID. Used for ads, as described in section 4.
  • Google Play billing. Used for Premium.

Metria does not ask for your contacts, microphone or precise location.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

What Metria stores stays on your phone until you delete it. Delete any saved measurement from History, or everything at once from Settings, then Delete all measurements. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Data collected by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies, and you can manage it from your Google Account.

10. Children

Metria is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your rights

You can ask us any question about your information, or make a complaint, by email. We will reply within 30 days. Because your measurements stay on your phone, we can't see, correct or delete them for you, but you can do all of that in the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how Metria handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches you.
